## History — key facts

**First Age:** Humans last to achieve hyperspace but came to dominate. Legacy: SI units and English as galaxy-wide standards. Ended by first hyperspace eclipse.

**Second Age:** Hyperspace travel restored but communication non-functional — only physical transport possible. The Intergalactic Postal Service held a monopoly on hyperspace cargo and post. Characterised by fragmentation, unethical research, abundant slavery, genetic engineering arms race. Ends with second eclipse.

**Earth's destruction:** Shortly after the first Age ended, the Thageis sent Negentropy Bombs (open a hyperspace portal extracting energy from normal space, propagating outward at C) intended as weather disruption. New technology was poorly understood — bombs detonated between Earth and the Sun, triggering a solar avalanche. Result: 85% of humans dead, atmosphere stripped ~50%, Earth no longer reachable by standard hyperspace (local solar environment disrupted). The Sun dimmed noticeably. The Bolon immediately surrounded the Thagei homeworld, executed the ruling class and responsible scientists, imposed democratic governance. Thageis have been constructive participants ever since.

**The Tribes:** Genetically modified and hybrid peoples evacuated by the Bolon at the end of the Second Age to a region with no habitable planets. Developed short-jump technology during the second eclipse (widely believed to be Bolon-assisted). Now a stable space-bound civilisation.

**Third Age (current):** Hyperspace research moves from empirical to theoretical. A-Z classification system established. Hendar compute layer (F-band) understood and programmable. Consensus: third eclipse unlikely but not impossible.

## Species

### The Bolon
- Oldest known hyperspace-active species, >1 million year recorded history
- Originate from a gas giant — life evolved on floating coral reef structures
- Cannot survive normal atmospheric pressure without protection; their "suits" are essentially small spacecraft — collections of high-pressure spheres
- Continued to travel and communicate normally during both eclipses using unclassified hyperspace bands
- Doctrine: observe and steer gently; intervene decisively when necessary, then withdraw. Do not seek control or occupation.
- D3 band: signals detected but not decoded; Bolon connection is speculation
- Ambassadors in most major systems; treated with wariness

### The Thageis
- One homeworld + colonised moon
- Under democratic governance post-Bolon intervention
- Constructive participants in galactic relations; the history is known

### The Tribes
- Genetically modified and hybrid peoples; built space-bound civilisation during second eclipse
- **Short-jump drives:** travel between stars in days to weeks without standard hyperspace access — huge tactical advantage during any eclipse or disruption
- Ships: very small, typically unpressurised — crew live in suits
- **Inner tribes:** rare encounters; strict religious/cultural law; capital punishment for breaking customs during first contact, sometimes delivered on-spot
- **Half-tribals:** the contact layer — trading, travelling near-border worlds, responsible for a portion of criminal activity in border space
- A tribal fleet that is not peaceful is a serious problem (short-jump in combat = tactical teleport within a system)

**Graham bands (P, Q, R)** are the source of exotic materials used in hull element production. Which band a foundry accesses determines what crystal properties are available — directly connecting band classification to the hull panel economy.

**Hendar (F-band):** All modern computing runs here, arranged as microservices. Ship computers, station infrastructure, galactic networks. A targeted F-band disruption could affect computation across a region.

## Hyperspace eclipses

Two in recorded history. Galaxy-wide events cutting off all standard hyperspace — travel, communication, most technology dependent on hyperspace bands. Bolon continued normally during both. Widely suspected to have assisted ending both. A third is considered unlikely by Third Age theory, but not impossible.

## Hyperspace anomalies

Regions of normal space with unusual proximity to hyperspace planes. Detectable with specialist scanners. Naturally occurring hyperspace crystals form in asteroid fields near strong anomalies — the first evidence hyperspace interaction was possible.

Different anomaly types → different crystal properties (determined by which hyperspace plane the region is near). A newly discovered anomaly's crystal properties are unknown until grown and analysed. **This discovery has genuine economic and scientific value** — worth selling, worth fighting over.

> *Gameplay: survey with specialist scanner, deploy seeding equipment, return weeks later to harvest. Career path with no scripting required.*

## Hyperspace crystals

Hyperspace effects are produced by **special crystals excited by laser**. Crystals are hyperspace-doped exotic materials: hyperspace subatomic particles attached to atomic nuclei, altering the electron cloud and the material's physical properties.

Crystal tuning is achieved by adjusting:
- Laser wavelength
- Polarisation
- Incoming angle
- Number of lasers (Vectors, usually 1–3) and their relative configuration

This is how the drive and all other hyperspace systems produce their effects.

Crystals are not interchangeable — different anomaly sources produce different affinities. Natural crystals near anomalies came first; technology bootstrapped from observation.

## The drive

- Creates a **standing wave of ionised metal ions** partially switched through A-band hyperspace
- Asymmetry in normal space produces thrust
- Fed by **metal rods** (reaction mass)
- Wave idles at all times when engine is on; thrust = wave **offset** adjustment
- Thrust ramps — not instant; fuel consumption scales with offset
- Both forward and reverse thrust from the same mechanism
- Rotation via gyroscopic effect — no separate thrusters needed
- Downward-thrust optional via slightly curved tube — most engines have this
- Exhausts **degenerate matter** (normal matter with hyperspace particles on nuclei)
- Exhaust deposits build up — periodic cleaning needed; strange crystals can form in deposits
- **Drive aligns to local gravitational field** → ship inherits reference frame of nearest large body → solves inter-system velocity discontinuity → natural speed cap in deep space (alignment becomes unstable at high offset in weak gravity)

## Alpha red

When two drives run together: one as `+`, one as `−`. Alpha red (A-band derived) flows between them. Nearly impossible to contain outside the space between engines — leaks as red streaks through air. A visible tell of ship power state. Can be guided through carbon crystals (graphene arrangements, diamond) to induce spin — provides extra rotational control.

## Shields

- Use **V-band**, Alpha or Theta sub-bands. Theta is superior.
- V-band also supports momentum dampening
- **J-b sub-band** produces donut vortices — effective anti-shield weapon type
- Shield always on once started; never binary. Capacitor % drives everything.
- Two per ship normally (top + bottom umbrellas), equippable separately

## Exotic materials & hull elements

Hull elements are produced from hyperspace-doped exotic materials (Graham band particles doped into atomic nuclei during crystal growth):
- Extremely strong and lightweight
- Hard to cut or post-process after formation
- **Must be produced in standard sizes from the outset**

Ships have geometric designs because **hull elements come in standard topological forms** — the manufacturing process determines this, not aesthetic choice. **The low-poly look is canon.** Players understand intuitively.

| Grade | Source | Availability |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| Standard | Common anomaly types | Everywhere |
| Military grade | Licensed foundries, specific anomaly types | Restricted, expensive |
| Exotic / rare | Specific known anomaly locations, often contested | Scarce, high value |

Panel condition is visible externally — ship history readable at a glance. Losing a ship means losing that patina.

## The hyperspace heat sink

Ships do not radiate waste heat into normal space (large thermal signature). Instead, waste heat is dumped via a hyperspace device transferring thermal energy into a separate hyperspace plane.

- Finite capacity — it fills up
- When **saturated**: dumps heat into realspace instantly — massive thermal spike + EM burst — every passive sensor in range lights up
- Pirate tactic: force a ship to run silent under pressure. Wait for the saturated vent.

---

## The emergency beacon

Every ship carries a mandatory beacon (cannot be removed):
- Single-use, requires expensive recharge after use
- Activates automatically on catastrophic hull failure — cannot be triggered voluntarily
- Performs one uncontrolled micro-jump to nearest registered station
- The jump experience: geometry that should not exist, colours outside normal spectrum, shapes in peripheral vision, 25–30 seconds, hard white, then a station
- **Costs sanity**

---

## What hyperspace is not

- Not a transport medium for normal matter
- Teleportation not available as general technology (beacon is tightly constrained)
- Hyperspace anomalies are not traversable — they are proximity regions, not holes
- Hendar layer is a computational substrate, not a messaging system
