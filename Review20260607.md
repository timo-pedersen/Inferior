### Suggested Focus for Next Step

The most valuable next chunk is the **power bus** — it's the dependency gate for nearly everything else. 
Without it, components tick in isolation, there's no starvation, no thermal spiral, no meaningful cold start, and the ship 
feels like a physics demo rather than a simulation. Specifically:

1. **`PowerBus`** — simple priority-sorted consumer list, distributes available watts per tick, records starvation state
2. **`Ship.PowerSystem`** — owns the bus + reactor + consumers, replaces `TickComponents` in `SpaceSimulation`
3. **Wire `PowerPriority` to the bus** — at minimum reactor → bus → engine, so thrust is power-dependent
4. **Cold start sequence** — emerges from the bus naturally once startup thresholds exist on components

This gives the ship a genuine dependency chain (no power → no thrust) and makes the instruments meaningful. It also validates the DataBus architecture under real load before adding more sensors. Everything else — thermal system, shields, damage — stacks cleanly on top of a working power bus.
